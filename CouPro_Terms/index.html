1. What Information Do We Collect?

In Short: We collect personal information that you provide to us.

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register on the Services, express an interest in obtaining information about us or our products and Services, when you participate in activities on the Services, or otherwise when you contact us.

Personal Information Provided by You. The personal information we collect may include:

  • Email addresses
  • Passwords
  • Phone numbers
  • Names (contact person names)
  • Business information (store name, store address, store type, business hours)
  • Images and photographs (store images, coupon images uploaded by you)
  • Transaction and redemption data (coupon details, redemption records, customer phone numbers for verification)

Sensitive Information. We do not process sensitive information.

Application Data

If you use our application(s), we also may collect the following information if you choose to provide us with access or permission:

  • Geolocation Information. We may request access or permission to track location-based information from your mobile device to provide certain location-based services. You may change our access or permissions in your device's settings.
  • Mobile Device Access. We may request access or permission to certain features from your mobile device, including your mobile device's camera and other features. You may change our access or permissions in your device's settings.

This information is primarily needed to maintain the security and operation of our application(s), for troubleshooting, and for our internal analytics and reporting purposes.

Error Monitoring and Performance Data

When you use our mobile application(s), we automatically collect technical data to improve stability and performance:

  • Error and crash reports — We use Sentry and similar services to capture error reports, stack traces, and device information when the app encounters crashes or errors. This helps us fix bugs and improve reliability.
  • Analytics and performance metrics — We use Google Firebase (Firebase Analytics and Firebase Performance Monitoring) to collect anonymized usage data, app performance metrics (e.g. screen load times, network request duration), and aggregated analytics. This helps us understand how the app is used and where to improve performance.

Data sent to these services is minimized and used only for error monitoring, analytics, and performance improvement. You can learn more about their practices via Sentry's and Google Firebase's respective privacy policies.

Merchant-Specific Information

If you register as a merchant on our Services, we collect additional information necessary to provide our merchant services, including:

  • Store/business name and address
  • Contact person information
  • Business hours and store type/category
  • Store images and logos
  • Coupon/promotion details (names, descriptions, quantities, validity periods, images)
  • Redemption records and transaction data
  • Business analytics (coupon views, redemption counts, usage statistics)

Customer Data for Redemption

When merchants process coupon redemptions, we may collect customer phone numbers solely for the purpose of verifying and completing the redemption transaction. This data is processed on behalf of the merchant and is subject to data minimization principles.

Google API

Our use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

All personal information that you provide to us must be true, complete, and accurate, and you must notify us of any changes to such personal information.

9. Controls for Do-Not-Track Features

Most web browsers and some mobile operating systems and mobile applications include a Do-Not-Track ("DNT") feature or setting you can activate to signal your privacy preference not to have data about your online browsing activities monitored and collected.

At this stage, no uniform technology standard for recognizing and implementing DNT signals has been finalized. As such, we do not currently respond to DNT browser signals or any other mechanism that automatically communicates your choice not to be tracked online. If a standard for online tracking is adopted that we must follow in the future, we will inform you about that practice in a revised version of this Privacy Notice.
